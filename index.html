[11] FAQ

Before we talk.

How do you price projects?

Fixed scope for well-defined builds, monthly for dedicated teams and retainers. Every fixed estimate follows a short discovery so the number holds.

Who owns the code?

You do. Repositories, infrastructure definitions and documentation are handed over and live in your accounts.

Do you sign an NDA?

Yes. We are happy to sign a mutual NDA before any detailed discussion.

Can you take over an existing product?

Yes. We start with a technical audit of the code, infrastructure and risks, then agree a stabilisation and roadmap plan.

How quickly can you start?

Usually within a few weeks, depending on team size. Tell us your timeline in the enquiry form and we will say what is realistic.
